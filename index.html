LogicGlade Privacy Policy

By Joytility Tech · Effective October 8, 2026

Joytility Tech (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides LogicGlade, an Android logic-puzzle app. This policy explains how information is handled when you use LogicGlade.

Information stored on your device

LogicGlade is designed for offline-first play. Puzzle progress, completed puzzles, collections, preferences, and gameplay statistics may be stored locally on your device so the app can function and remember your progress. These records are not transmitted to a Joytility Tech account or server as part of the app's core gameplay.

Personal information and accounts

LogicGlade does not require you to create a Joytility Tech account or provide your name, email address, or other personal contact information to play. We do not include advertising or behavioral advertising trackers in the planned core experience.

Purchases and Google Play

If you purchase optional puzzle packs through Google Play, Google handles payment processing and may process purchase and transaction information under its own policies. LogicGlade may receive purchase status and product entitlement information needed to unlock or restore purchased content. We do not receive your full payment-card details from Google Play.

Internet access and third parties

Core puzzles and locally saved progress are designed to work offline. Google Play services may be involved when purchases, purchase restoration, app distribution, or updates are used. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share locally stored puzzle progress with advertisers. Purchase-related information may be processed by Google Play to provide purchase functionality.

Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until removed through available app or Android storage controls, or uninstalling the app. Removing local app data may erase puzzle progress and collections. Google Play purchase records and entitlements may remain associated with your Google account according to Google's policies. Uninstalling the app does not itself delete Google's transaction records.

Children's privacy

LogicGlade does not require personal information to play. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through the app. Parents and guardians can contact us with privacy concerns.

Security

We aim to minimize information handling by keeping core gameplay data on your device. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if app functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Joytility Tech at joytilitytech@gmail.com.
